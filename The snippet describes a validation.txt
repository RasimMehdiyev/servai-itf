The snippet describes a validation framework for robust input reconstruction in the presence of system uncertainty and measurement noise. The uncertainty is represented through alternative assumptions on the structure of the frequency response function (FRF) covariance matrix. Authors position the validation across two axes: (1) how covariance of input structure affect reconstruction accuracy under a simulated uncertainty model and (2) whether an optimal sensor placement strategy can reduce the sensor set without degrading reconstruction accuracy.
Both are evaluated on synthetic and measured vibration data form and industrial end-of-line rig.
